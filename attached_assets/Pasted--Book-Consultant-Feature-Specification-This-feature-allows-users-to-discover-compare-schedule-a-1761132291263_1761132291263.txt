💻 Book Consultant Feature Specification
This feature allows users to discover, compare, schedule, and pay for on-demand or scheduled consultant services—in-person or virtual—across healthcare, wellness, home services, and professional help.

2. Booking Flows (User Journeys)
🚀 Standard Flow (In-Person Service)
Discovery→Provider Profile→Select Service/Slot→Add Address/Instructions→Confirm Price/Extras→Payment→Booking Confirmed→Live Status & Tracking→Service Complete→Rating & Invoice
📞 Virtual Consult Flow
Book Slot→Secure Video Link / Phone Call→Reminder→Session Live→Receipt & Follow-up Notes
🔄 Edge Case Flows
Multi-stop/Composite: Allows for combined services (e.g., Nurse + Lab Pick-up) in one linked booking.
Corporate/Bulk: Recurring schedules (e.g., daily nurse, weekly tutor) managed under a single corporate account.
3. Page-by-Page Requirements
Page / Component	Key Requirements & Elements	Filters & Sorts
Home / Discover	- Category Strip: Icons with quick promises (e.g., "Home visit in 60-90 min"). - Search Bar: Provider name, specialty, service. - Saved Searches & Recent Bookings.	Quick Filters: "Nearby," "Top rated," "Available now," "Price range," "Virtual only."
Search & Filters	- Map/List Toggle for in-person services. - Faceted Filters (left drawer/modal).	Faceted Filters: Category, Sub-specialty, Rating, Price, Availability (ASAP/Today/Later), Language, Gender, Verified Badge, Distance, Insurance Accepted (Doctors). Sort Options: Relevance, Rating, Price, Distance, Next Available Slot.
Provider Profile	- Photo, Name, Designation, Verified Badges (ID/License). - Experience, Average Rating & Reviews. - Services Offered (packages, duration, price, inclusions, add-ons). - Availability Calendar (real-time slot view, virtual vs. in-person toggle). - Policies (Cancellation, Refund, Safety Notes). - CTAs: "Book now," "Message provider" (chat), "Call" (masked).	
Booking Page (Critical)	- Slot Selection: ASAP (nearest free slot) or Scheduled (calendar picker). - Address Selector: Home/Office/New (map pin, multi-address option). - Attachments: Prescription upload (doctors), photos of issue (plumber). - Price Breakdown: Base fee, travel fee, taxes, addons, surge, gratuity option. - Payment Options: Prepaid (Card/UPI/Wallet/BNPL) or Pay at Service (with optional refundable deposit). - Promo/Coupon entry.	
Live Status & Tracking	- In-person: Live map, ETA, masked contact options, cancel/reschedule window. - Virtual: Countdown timer, Secure video link with Join button, pre-call test. - On-Demand (e.g., Plumber): Real-time updates (Diagnosis Started, Parts Required, Extra Quote Required).	
Service Completion & POD	- Provider marks complete, captures Proof of Delivery (POD) via photo/signature. - Customer confirms or flags issue. - Invoice & final payment settlement (if pay-on-service). - Option to Tip Provider.	
History & Reorder	- Booking history with Rebook button. - Invoice download, chat transcript, prescriptions/reports attached.	
Ratings & Feedback	- Star rating + Structured reasons (e.g., arrived late, quality). - Attach photo. - Auto-compensation engine linked to negative flags.	
4. Operational & Compliance Requirements
🛡️ Safety, Compliance & Verification
Verification: Mandatory ID and license verification (Doctors, Nurses, Technicians). Background checks and periodic re-verification for home-health workers.
SOS: In-app SOS for both provider and customer. Emergency escalation workflows for medical emergencies.
Medical Data: GDPR/HIPAA-like controls for secure storage of prescriptions and health data; explicit user consent required.
Age Checks: Ensure ID capture for services requiring age verification (e.g., certain salon products).
⚙️ Admin & Operations
Provider Management: Verification dashboard, onboarding queue, quality audits, pricing & commission engine.
Booking Management: Live booking map, manual assign/reassign, dispute queue, refunds/chargebacks dashboard.
💳 Payments, Pricing & Commissions
Pricing Models: Configurable by category (Flat fee + distance, Hourly rate, Appointment fee, Per test price).
Deposit: Option to capture a refundable deposit for high-value or pay-at-service bookings.
Commission: Flexible commission split (percent + fixed) configurable by service category.
B2B: Corporate billing and invoicing capabilities.
Insurance: Integration for third-party payment/insurance acceptance (doctors/medical tests).
5. Edge Cases & Business Rules
Rule	Description
No-Show / Late	Define auto-refund thresholds and free reschedule windows for both user and provider lateness/no-shows.
Multi-Provider Dependency	Dependency Management: If Doctor A orders Lab Test B, the system links the bookings (A → B) and prioritizes Lab B slot selection based on Doctor A's availability.
Extra Quote	Provider can raise an Extra Quote (for parts/repair costs) on-the-fly; user must explicitly accept before work continues.
Emergencies	Block immediate booking for non-emergency providers during severe health queries and display an Emergency Hotline number.
Cancellations	Tiered cancellation fees based on time to appointment (e.g., Free within 12 hours, 50% fee within 2 hours).
Minimums	Enforce minimum booking durations or minimum charges for house calls (e.g., 2-hour minimum for a handyman).
6. Acceptance Criteria (QA-Ready)
Check	Success Condition
Booking Creation	Must return a unique booking_id and confirm the available slot within 5 seconds.
Verification	Verified badge is only displayed when all required documents are uploaded and manually/systematically approved.
Slot Conflict	The system must prevent double-booking for the same provider at the exact same time slot.
Payments	Successful payments create a paid booking and generate an invoice. Failed payments must not create a booking (prevent duplicates).
Live Updates	Provider arrival/status updates must show live in the user timeline within 15 seconds of the provider action.
Proof of Delivery	Booking cannot be marked complete without capturing the required POD (photo/signature) for specified categories (e.g., Home Repairs, Nurse, Driver).
