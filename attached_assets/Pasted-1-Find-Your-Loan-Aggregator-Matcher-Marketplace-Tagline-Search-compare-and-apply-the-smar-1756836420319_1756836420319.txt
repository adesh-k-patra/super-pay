1. Find Your Loan — Aggregator, Matcher & Marketplace
Tagline: Search, compare, and apply — the smartest way to find the right loan for your purpose.
What it is (short)
An in-app loan marketplace that aggregates partner offers, ranks them by trust & fit for a user’s profile, and lets users compare, prefill, and apply — while providing lenders with analytics and premium placement.
Why it matters
* Highest direct monetization potential (lead-fees, revenue share, API subscriptions).
* Increases conversion by surfacing best-fit offers for each user.
* Improves retention: users return to compare and track applications.
* Builds brand trust with transparent ranking and “InCred-backed” badges.
Primary user flow
Home → Tap “Find Your Loan” → Purpose + Amount + Tenure + Quick Profile (or auto-prefill from MyReport) → Ranked results (Diamond→Bronze) → Compare / Apply → Consent & Pre-filled Application → KYC upload → Submit → Track in Applications hub.
Key screens & UI elements
* Landing / Quick Match: Purpose tiles, amount slider, tenure selector, “Use MyReport” toggle, CTA “Find matches”.
* Results List: Compact card with badge, APR, EMI, total cost, processing fee, approval speed, “How this fits you” microcopy, CTAs: Compare / Apply / Save.
* Compare Modal: Up to 3 lenders side-by-side, differences highlighted (APR, fees, docs), “Best for you” AI callout.
* Product Detail: Fee table, amortization, docs required, lender reviews, sponsored disclosure.
* Application Flow: Pre-filled form, consent screen, KYC uploader, submit, confirmation.
* Applications Hub: Status, lender contact, required next steps, appeals.
* Lender Portal: Lead inbox, filters, lead scoring, conversion analytics.
Sub-features / capability list
* Trust Badge system (Diamond → Bronze) using composite scores.
* Predicted Approval Score personalized per user.
* “Best for you” explanation card (one-line reason).
* Organic vs Sponsored labeling & toggle.
* “InCred-backed” partner badge for negotiated benefits.
* EMI & total cost calculator and amortization preview.
* Prefill via MyReport & consented bureau data.
* Export/share comparison snapshot (PDF / image).
* Partner lead webhook & analytics endpoints.
Data & integrations
* MyReport (profile + credit data).
* Bureau APIs (CIBIL/Experian) for eligibility checks (consent required).
* Lender APIs for offers, eligibility checks, and lead intake.
* Payment & KYC providers (Aadhaar, PAN, eKYC providers).
* Analytics events and attribution tracking.
Monetization paths
* Lead fee (CPL) / revenue share on disbursal.
* Sponsored placement / CPC (clearly labeled).
* Lender subscriptions for analytics & prioritized leads.
* “Premium match” for MyReport subscribers (better filters, negotiated rates).
Privacy, compliance & safety
* Explicit consent screens before sharing PII.
* Data minimization (only required fields to partner).
* Audit trail of consents & partner acceptance.
* Partner onboarding with compliance checks & periodic audits.
Success metrics (KPIs)
* Compare → Apply conversion rate.
* Marketplace revenue (CPL / % origination).
* Repeat users (month-over-month).
* Average session length on marketplace screens.
* NPS lift among marketplace users.
Acceptance criteria (examples)
* Search and results render with badges and “Best for you” text.
* Prefill works from MyReport with explicit consent.
* Apply flow sends leads to partner webhook and Application Hub shows status.
* Sponsored labeling present and clear.

2. MyReport — Deep Personalized Financial Roadmap (Premium)
Tagline: A weekly-updated, data-driven roadmap that makes your financial goals unavoidable.
What it is (short)
A premium, subscriber-only financial health product that aggregates bureau + bank + in-app data to produce prioritized actions, projected eligibility, scenario simulations, and automated reminders.
Why it matters
* Primary subscription revenue driver.
* Directly improves users’ eligibility and propensity to convert.
* Powers personalization across Find Your Loan, MyPath and Coach.
Primary user flow
Subscribe → Authorize data pulls (bureau + open banking) → MyReport builds and shows Snapshot → Prioritized “What to Improve” list → Scenario simulator & timelineed actions → Weekly updates and reminders → CTA to apply or ask Coach.
Sections & pages
* Subscribe / Pricing: benefits, sample pages, FAQs.
* Snapshot Dashboard: credit score, debt summary, eligibility score, quick wins.
* What to Improve: ranked actionable items (Debt, DTI, Utilization, Account Age, Delinquencies).
* How to Improve: step-by-step timeline with estimated score deltas and ETA.
* Projected Loan Progression: visual graphs showing eligibility by date based on actions.
* Automations & Reminders: scheduled payments, alerts, calendar sync.
* Export / Share: secure PDF export & share to advisors (consented).
Core features
* Action cards with estimated score impact and confidence band.
* Scenario simulator: “If I pay ₹X, score ↑ by Y”.
* Auto-reminders & scheduling tools to execute quick wins.
* Privacy controls to manage data sources and sharing.
Algorithm & modeling notes
* Use historical bureau models + heuristics for estimated score deltas.
* Confidence bands (low/medium/high) shown for each estimate.
* Simulation engine considers payment schedules, utilization changes, and time decay.
Data & integrations
* Bureau pulls (CIBIL/Experian) with consent.
* Open-banking account data for debt & income checks.
* In-app events & payment history.
* Find Your Loan signals for lender-fit models.
Monetization & tiers
* Basic subscriber tier: snapshot + weekly PDF.
* Pro tier: full timeline, scenario simulator, priority Coach messages, advanced analytics.
* Partner APIs for anonymized market insights sold to lenders.
Privacy & compliance
* Explicit opt-in for each data source.
* End-to-end encryption for stored bureau & bank data.
* Granular revoke access controls for users.
KPIs
* Subscriber conversion rate from free users.
* MRR & ARPU.
* Churn rate for MyReport subscribers.
* Conversion rate from MyReport → Find Your Loan applications.
Acceptance criteria (examples)
* MyReport shows snapshot + 5 ranked actions with estimated impacts.
* Scenario simulator updates score estimate when user changes input.
* Exports produce downloadable PDF with user’s consent metadata.

4. InCredSecure — Instant Fraud Alert Checker
Tagline: Trust before you transact — scan any link, message or screenshot in seconds.
What it is (short)
An AI + rules-based scanner that inspects pasted links, uploaded screenshots, or suspicious messages and returns an Authenticity Score (0–10), evidence, and clear remediation steps.
Why it matters
* Protects users from phishing and fraud.
* Lowers fraud-linked support costs and brand risk.
* Creates viral “share-to-warn” flows and trust signals.
Inputs & detection vectors
* Paste URL / paste message / upload screenshot (OCR).
* Resolve redirect chains, expand shorteners, check SSL certs, WHOIS age, blacklist checks, typosquat detection, UPI/pay link heuristics, suspicious query parameters.
Score & outputs
* 9–10: Highly Safe (green)
* 5–8: Proceed with Caution (amber)
* <5: Scam Detected (red) Each result shows evidence items, risk tags, confidence %, recommended action (Block / Report / Validate / Open in Sandbox), and an “Explain” view.
Primary user flow
Home → Tap InCredSecure → Paste link / paste message / upload screenshot → Scanner runs → Result page with score, evidence, remediation CTA (Block / Report / Share / Open). User can save scan history and share warning cards.
Key screens & UI elements
* Scanner Input: URL, plaintext, or screenshot upload UI with examples.
* Result Page: prominent score dial, color-coded action card, collapsible evidence list, share and report buttons.
* History: saved scans, user reports, moderation status.
* Trust Ops Dashboard: aggregated signals, trending threats, appeals queue.
Technology & capabilities
* Redirect resolver & URL expansion engine.
* SSL/TLS verification and certificate mismatch detection.
* WHOIS & domain age lookups.
* Typosquat fuzzy matching and brand list.
* OCR for screenshots + NLP classifier for message semantics.
* Threat intelligence & blacklist integrations.
* Sandbox webview for safe previewing.
Actions & remediation
* Local blocking (in-app warning).
* Report to InCred Trust Ops and optionally to national CERT / payment networks.
* Share-to-contact or social with templated warning cards.
* Open in an isolated sandbox for advanced users.
Privacy & compliance
* Redact sensitive data in screenshot logs unless user consents.
* Opt-out for telemetry.
* Appeal / whitelist flow for false positives.
KPIs
* Reduction in fraud incidents among users who use the tool.
* Number of share-to-warn actions (virality signal).
* Precision/recall metrics of classifier.
* Reduction in fraud-related support tickets.
Acceptance criteria (examples)
* Scanner returns score and evidence for URLs and screenshots.
* Users can block/report directly from result page.
* History of scans is available and shareable.

5. InCred Coach — AI Financial Coach (Context-Aware Q&A)
Tagline: Your financial coach, built from your data — clear, contextual, and instantly actionable.
What it is (short)
An AI chat assistant that uses the user’s MyReport and in-app data to answer personalized finance questions, provide action steps, and link to relevant products and learning.
Why it matters
* Scales human-quality advice and reduces support load.
* Drives engagement and nudges users into paid products.
* Improves conversion via contextual guidance (e.g., “which lenders to try next”).
Where it appears
* Inline in MyReport (chat icon).
* Embedded inside MyPath and Find Your Loan (contextual queries).
* Dedicated Coach tab for exploratory Q&A.
Example interactions
* “Why is my utilization high?” → breakdown of card balances, utilization % per card, actionable next steps (increase limit, pay ₹X).
* “How much do I need to settle to raise my score by 10 points?” → simulator + playbook + product link.
* “Which lenders will likely approve me after this change?” → links to Find Your Loan matches.
Architecture & safety
* Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) using MyReport vectors, product docs, and FAQs.
* Source attribution for factual claims (e.g., “Estimate based on X model; confidence: medium”).
* Hallucination guardrails and legal/regulatory disclaimers for sensitive queries.
* Fine-tuned on anonymized advisor scripts.
UI features
* Contextual answer cards with “Next step” CTAs.
* Inline CTA: Apply / Schedule / Book 1-on-1.
* Save answers to user’s Plan or Tasks (MyPath).
* Escalate to human advisor (Creator 1-on-1) with one tap.
Privacy & compliance
* Coach uses only consented data; sensitive advice includes disclaimers.
* Logs anonymized for improvement; user can opt out.
KPIs
* Chat deflection (support ticket reduction).
* Engagement minutes with Coach.
* Conversion from Coach suggestions → product actions.
Acceptance criteria (examples)
* Coach returns actionable answers with at least one CTA in-context.
* Answers show source attribution and confidence level.
* Users can save advice to MyPath tasks.
