1. InCredSecure — Instant Fraud Alert Checker

Tagline: Trust before you transact — scan any link, message or screenshot in seconds.
What it does (short): A lightning-fast, AI-powered scanner that inspects pasted links, uploaded screenshots, or suspicious messages and returns a simple Authenticity Score (0–10) plus clear reasons and remediation steps.
User flow (3 steps): Home → Tap InCredSecure → Paste link / paste message / upload screenshot → Instant result page with score, reasons, and “Report / Block / Share” actions.
Scoring / outputs: 9–10 = Highly Safe; 5–8 = Proceed with Caution; <5 = Scam Detected. Each result lists evidence (domain age, redirect chain, SSL issues, WHOIS flags, blacklist matches, typosquat risk, suspicious UPI/pay link patterns) and a color-coded action card.
Impact: Increases customer trust, reduces fraud-related churn, lowers support costs, and turns safety into a brand differentiator with huge viral potential (share-to-warning flows).
Why it matters: Protecting users from scams positions InCred as guardian of customers’ financial health — a powerful brand trust engine that drives both acquisition (word-of-mouth) and retention.

2. MyPath — Interactive Rejection-to-Roadmap Experience

Tagline: Rejection is the start of a plan, not the end of the road.
What it does (short): Converts loan rejections into actionable guidance: a personalized, interactive dashboard that tells each user why they were declined and exactly what to do next.
User flow (4 steps): Application → If rejected, full-screen modal invites user to view MyPath → Personalized dashboard showing Credit Score, Loan Eligibility Score, and ranked “Areas to Improve” → Clickable “Learn More” actions linking to targeted learning or product suggestions.
Key elements: Brief explainer cards (e.g., “High DTI: ₹X, effect: –Y points”), prioritized fixes, estimated impact (e.g., “Settle ₹5,000 → +25 score in ~45 days”), and quick product links (credit-builder loan, secured EMI).
Impact: Reduces funnel leakage, increases re-application rates, raises paid upsell conversion to MyReport, and improves NPS by transforming a negative moment into a value-packed micro-experience.
Why it matters: Customers want the why and the how. MyPath gives both — increasing trust and paving the path from rejection → engagement → subscription.

3. MyReport — Deep Personalized Financial Roadmap (Premium)

Tagline: A weekly-updated, data-driven roadmap that makes your financial goals unavoidable.
What it does (short): Subscriber-only, comprehensive financial health report with smart recommendations, timelineed actions, and projected improvements in eligibility & terms.
User flow (4 steps): Subscribe to InCredible Wealth → View MyReport → Sections: Current Snapshot → What to Improve (ranked) → How to Improve (step-by-step timeline) → Projected Loan Progression graph.
Report sections: CIBIL & bureau details, income breakdown, debt summary, prioritized actions (with estimated score impact), scheduled reminders, and automated progress tracking.
Impact: Core subscription product — drives MRR, raises LTV, and gives the sales/marketing team a clear value proposition to convert free users.
Why it matters: MyReport is the north star feature: measurable impact on customers’ financial outcomes (and the app’s revenue). It’s the premium value users will happily pay for because it replaces confusion with a clear, action-first plan.

Tagline: Search, compare, and apply — the smartest way to find the right loan for your purpose.
What it does (short): A one-stop loan marketplace inside the InCred app that aggregates offers from every partner lender and loan app, ranks them by trust and fit, and matches customers to the best option for their purpose — quickly and transparently.
User flow (4 steps):

Home → Tap Find Your Loan.

Enter purpose (Education / Home / Auto / Business / Personal / Medical / Travel / EMI), amount, tenure, and a quick profile (salary band or pre-fill from MyReport).

See a ranked list with clear badges (Diamond / Platinum / Gold / Silver / Bronze), filter/sort (lowest rate, lowest EMI, fastest approval, highest approval rate), and “Best for you” AI match.

Tap Compare for side-by-side breakdown → Apply (pre-filled application or redirect) → track status in-app.

Ranking & Trust Model:

Tier badges (Diamond → Bronze): based on a composite Trust Score: historical approval rates for similar profiles, transparent rates & fees, partner KYC & compliance posture, user reviews, time-to-disbursal, and external reputation (where available).

Why these matter: Diamond = best combination of trust, competitive pricing and fast approvals; Bronze = newer / niche players with varying credentials. Badges are algorithmically assigned and human-reviewed for accuracy.

Key UI elements & signals:

Compact comparison cards showing: interest rate (APR), EMI estimate, total cost, processing fee, eligibility quick-check, approval speed, and tier badge.

“How this fits you” callout (e.g., “Best for low-income salaried applicants — likely pre-approval in 24–48 hrs”).

Clear toggle: Organic vs Sponsored results (transparency for users). Sponsored partners may appear in a dedicated slot but never hide higher-quality organic matches unless explicitly promoted with consent.

“InCred-backed” badge for partners offering exclusive benefits (reduced processing fee, priority underwriting) via co-marketing deals.

Monetization (Skyscanner-style marketplace):

Referral / Revenue-share: When a user applies or converts after being redirected, InCred earns a lead fee or shared revenue on the loan (CPL / % of loan origination).

CPC / Featured Placements: Partners can pay for prominent placement in “Sponsored” slots, clearly labeled.

Subscription / API Access for Lenders: Lenders pay for analytics, prioritized access to matched leads, and enhanced conversion tooling (pre-fill APIs, faster underwriting channels).

Premium Search Enhancements for Users: Allow MyReport subscribers to unlock advanced match filters (predicted approval score, guaranteed fee waivers from partners, or pre-negotiated interest rates).

Safety, Compliance & Transparency:

Full disclosure of fees, APR, and whether a result is sponsored.

Privacy-first data sharing: explicit user consent before passing PII to partners; pre-fill only via user opt-in.

Regulatory guardrails for partner onboarding (KYC checks, compliance audits).

Product impact & KPIs:

Acquisition: higher app installs and discovery traffic via organic search & aggregator marketing.

Conversion: increase in quality leads (CPL) and higher affiliate revenue.

Retention: users return to compare offers and track applications.

Monetization: CAC recovery via lead sales, increased ARPU via marketplace fees and premium features.

Trust: measurable uplift in NPS from transparent comparison + badge system.
