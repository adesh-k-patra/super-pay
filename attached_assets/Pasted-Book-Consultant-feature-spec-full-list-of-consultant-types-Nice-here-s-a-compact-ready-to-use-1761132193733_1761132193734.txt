1 — What this feature does (one line)
Allow users to discover, compare, schedule and pay for on-demand or scheduled consultant services — in-person or virtual — across healthcare, wellness, home services and professional help.

3 — Booking flows (user journeys)
Discovery → Provider profile → Select service (package/slot) → Add address & instructions → Confirm price & extras → Payment or pay-at-service → Booking confirmed → Provider enroute / session live → Service complete → Rating & invoice.
Virtual consult: Book slot → secure video link / phone call → reminder → session → receipt & follow-up notes.
Multi-stop / multi-provider: e.g., nurse + lab pick-up in one booking (composite booking).
Corporate / bulk booking: recurring schedules (daily nurse, weekly tutor).
4 — Page-by-page requirements
Home / Discover
Category strip with icons and delivery/visit promises (e.g., “Home visit in 60–90 min”, “Instant virtual consult”).
Search bar (provider name, specialty, service).
Quick filters for “Nearby”, “Top rated”, “Available now”, “Price range”, “Virtual only”.
Saved searches & recent bookings.
Search & Filters
Faceted filters (left drawer or modal): category, sub-specialty, rating, price, availability (ASAP / Today / Later), languages, gender preference (for healthcare / salon), verified badge, distance, insurance accepted (for doctors).
Sort options: Relevance, Rating, Price (low-high), Distance, Next available slot.
Map/list toggle for in-person services.
Provider / Consultant Profile
Photo, name, designation, certifications, verified badges (ID / license), years experience, languages, average rating & reviews.
Services offered (packages) with duration, price, inclusions, add-ons.
Availability calendar (real-time slot view), virtual vs in-person toggle.
Policies: cancellation, refund, reschedule, safety notes.
Gallery (images), sample documents (certificates), FAQs, sample consultation notes.
“Book now”, “Message provider” (chat), “Call” (masked number), “Share profile”.
Service Detail (package)
Duration, what’s included, preparation required, limits (e.g., max 2 people), equipment needed, payment type, required documents (prescription).
Add-ons (oxygen monitor, extra time, home collection kit, cleaning supplies).
Booking page (critical)
Slot selection: ASAP (nearest free slot) or scheduled (calendar + time picker).
Address selector: home / office / new (map pin & geocode). Option for multi-address (pick-up/drop).
Contact details: name, phone, notes, access instructions.
Attachments: prescription upload (doctors), photos of issue (plumber/electrician), measurements (furniture/photographer).
Price breakdown: base fee, travel fee, taxes, addons, surge, gratuity option. Show total.
Payment options: prepaid (card/UPI/wallet/BNPL), pay at service (collect cash / card on delivery). If pay-on-service, capture intent and possible refundable deposit.
Promo/coupon entry, apply & show savings.
Confirmation CTA (fixed bottom button), shows estimated provider arrival time.
Booking confirmation
Booking ID, ETA/to-be-confirmed, provider assignment status (unassigned / assigned / enroute).
Option: “Request preferred provider” (if available), share booking, cancel/reschedule CTA with policy.
Live status & tracking
For in-person: live map with provider location, ETA, contact provider (masked), cancel/reschedule window until arrival. For driver/chauffeur bookings also show trip status.
For virtual: countdown timer, video link with join button, pre-call test (mic/camera).
For on-demand tasks (electrician/plumber), allow real-time updates: diagnosis started, parts required, extra quote required.
Service completion & POD
Confirmation of task done: provider marks complete, captures proof (photo/signature), customer confirms or flags issue.
For recurring services: scheduling next appointment.
Invoice & payment settlement (if pay-on-service capture final payment). Option to tip provider.
History & Reorder
Booking history with rebook button, invoice download, chat transcript, prescriptions and reports (medical tests), prescriptions attached in profile.
Ratings & Feedback
Star rating + structured reason (arrived late, quality, behaviour), attach photo. Auto-compensation engine for certain negative flags.

Profile & verification onboarding (ID, license, certificates, police verification if needed).
Availability toggle & calendar sync (Google / Calendar).
Accept/decline bookings with short reason codes. Auto-assign rules when offline.
Navigation (integrated maps), arrival check-in, start/complete actions, capture POD (photos / signatures), collect payment (if COD).
Earnings dashboard, payout requests, trip history, support chat, shift scheduling.

6 — Admin & operations
Provider verification dashboard, onboarding queue, quality audits.
Live booking map, manual assign/reassign, dispute queue, refunds & chargebacks dashboard.
Pricing & commission engine, promo management, KYC/settlement for payouts.
Reports: utilization, response time, cancellations, test conversion (medical tests).
7 — Payments, pricing & commissions
Pricing models per category: flat fee + distance/travel fee (salon, driver), hourly rate (driver, tutor), appointment fee (doctor), per test price (lab).
Deposit concept for high value bookings or pay-at-service risk.
Commission split: percent + fixed, configurable by category.
Corporate billing & invoices for B2B bookings.
Insurance & third-party payment (for doctors/medical tests) integration where applicable.
8 — Safety, compliance & verification (essential)
ID & license verification for doctors, nurse, technicians. Background checks & periodic re-verification for home-health workers.
Display verified badges for completed checks.
In-app SOS for both provider & customer. Emergency escalation workflows (ambulance / authorities) for medical emergencies.
For medical bookings: secure storage for prescriptions and health data, GDPR/HIPAA-like controls; explicit consent for storing medical data.
For services requiring age checks (salon with adult-only products), ensure ID capture.
9 — Filters & search tuning (perfect filter menu)
Category-specific filters:
Doctors: specialty, consultation type (virtual/in-person), insurance accepted, gender preference, experience, hospital affiliation.
Salon: service type (haircut, styling, makeup), gender, price range, home service vs salon, stylist experience.
Driver: vehicle type, hourly / point-to-point, driver rating, luggage capacity.
Nurse: qualifications (RN/ANM), languages, shift length (hourly/daily), medical experience.
Medical test: test type, sample pickup, partner lab, turnaround time.
Global filters: price, rating, availability (ASAP / next 24 hrs), verified.
10 — Edge cases & business rules
No-show & late arrival policies: auto-refund thresholds, free reschedule windows.
Multi-provider dependency: e.g., doctor orders a lab test — booking links to lab sample pickup with dependency management.
Parts & extra repair costs (plumber/electrician): provider can raise extra-quote; user must accept before continuing.
Medical emergencies: block immediate booking for non-emergency providers and display emergency hotline.
Cancellations: tiered fees based on time to appointment; free cancellation window.
Minimum booking durations / minimum charges for house calls.

12 — Acceptance criteria (QA-ready)
Booking creation: returns booking_id and available slot within 5s.
Verification: verified badge only when docs uploaded & approved.
Slot conflict: double-book prevention per provider.
Payments: successful payments create paid booking & generate invoice. Failed payments must not create duplicate bookings.
Provider arrival updates must show live in user timeline within 15s of provider action.
POD: booking cannot be marked complete without proof for specified categories (e.g., home repairs, nurse, driver).
