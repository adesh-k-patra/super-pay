Page 2: Eligibility & The 4-Step Onboarding Flow
✅ Basic Eligibility Criteria
To qualify for a pre-approved limit on Instapay Credit UPI, you must meet the following general criteria:

Citizenship: Indian Citizen

Age: 18 years or older

Credit Profile: Minimum CIBIL Score of 730 is highly recommended. (Applicants with limited or no credit history may still be considered based on UPI transaction history and Instapay relationship score).

KYC: Valid PAN and Aadhaar linked to your registered mobile number.

Bank Account: An active UPI-linked bank account with any partner bank (required for linking the credit line).

🚀 Instant Onboarding in 4 Simple Steps
The entire application process is 100% digital and takes less than 3 minutes for pre-approved users.

Check Offer: Open the Instapay app and navigate to the "Credit UPI" section. View your pre-approved limit offered by Innovate Bank.

Verify & Consent: Review the Key Fact Statement (KFS) including interest rates and fees. Complete Aadhaar-based OTP verification for e-KYC and digitally sign the Credit Agreement.

Link Account: The Innovate Bank Credit Line Account will be automatically linked to your Instapay UPI ID.

Set PIN: Set a dedicated, unique 6-digit UPI PIN for all Instapay Credit UPI transactions. Your credit line is now active!

Page 3: Usage, Repayment, & Mandatory Conditions
💳 How to Use Your Credit Line
Using Instapay Credit UPI is just like making a normal UPI payment, with one key difference: you select the funding source.

Scan: Scan any merchant UPI QR code (e.g., at a grocery store, petrol pump, or online checkout).

Enter Amount: Input the amount you need to pay.

Select Source: On the payment confirmation screen, change the default debit account to "Innovate Bank Credit UPI (Limit: ₹X,XXX)".

Authenticate: Enter your dedicated Instapay Credit UPI PIN to complete the transaction.

🛑 Usage Restrictions (Mandatory Conditions)
As mandated by regulatory guidelines, the Instapay Credit UPI can ONLY be used for:

P2M (Person-to-Merchant) Transactions: Payments to registered commercial merchants (online or offline).

Restricted Transactions: P2P (Person-to-Person) transfers, payments to other lending platforms, cash withdrawals (ATM or merchant), or investments (like Mutual Funds/IPOs) are STRICTLY PROHIBITED.

🗓 Repayment Cycle & Grace Period
Billing Date: Your statement will be generated on the 1st of every month.

Due Date: You have a 15-day grace period to repay the full outstanding amount. The due date is the 16th of the month.

Interest-Free Period: All utilized funds are interest-free if repaid in full by the due date. Interest begins accruing only after the due date or from the date of withdrawal if the payment is subsequently converted into an EMI.
