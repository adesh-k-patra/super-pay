MyCredit Report
1. Snapshot Dashboard (Landing Page)

What users see immediately:

Current Credit Score (with a trust badge).

Debt Summary (total active loans).

Loan Eligibility Score (% chance of approval).

Quick Buttons:

“View Timeline”

“Check Loans & EMIs”

“Improve My Score”

“Download Report”

👉 Designed for instant clarity — users know their financial standing at a glance.

2. Credit Score Timeline

Graph-based history + future projection of credit score.

Shows key reasons for past ups and downs (e.g., missed EMI, high utilization, on-time payments).

Predictive projection: “If you follow roadmap → Score may rise to 780 by Dec 2025.”

Menu on top:

Calculator (simulate payoff/utilization).

Download Report.

👉 Makes credit score transparent + easy to understand.

3. Loan & Debt Dashboard

All loans listed with duration, EMI, due dates, payment history.

Graphs & Stats:

Total Interest Paid.

Remaining Principal.

Total Installments (done vs pending).

Missed Payments (red flags).

Personalized Loan Calculator:

Prepayment impact → “Pay ₹20,000 now → Save ₹5,200 interest + close 4 months earlier.”

Shortening tenure vs lowering EMI calculator.

👉 Helps users control loans better and avoid surprises.

4. Actionable Roadmap

Personalized “What to Improve” list (ranked by urgency & impact).

Ex: Reduce utilization → +30 points.

Ex: Clear overdue loan → +50 points.

How to Improve: Step-by-step timeline with dates + projected score change.

Visual Progress Graph: “Eligibility will rise to 90% by June if actions are followed.”

👉 Turns advice into clear steps users can actually do.

5. Scenario Simulator calulator (Interactive Tool)

“What if” playground for financial planning.

Examples:

“If I repay ₹50,000 on Loan A → My score improves by +40.”

“If I take a new credit card → Approval chance drops 10% short-term.”

Instant recalculation → Graph updates live.

👉 Helps users test choices before making them.

6. MyCoach (AI Assistant)

Floating chatbot across MyCredit pages.

Functions:

Explain score numbers in simple words.

Suggest next actions (“Pay EMI on time this week for +5 points”).

Set reminders (“Remind me 2 days before my EMI is due”).

👉 Acts like a personal credit trainer inside the app.

7. Secure Export & Sharing

Options:

Download Snapshot PDF (quick version).

Download Full Report PDF (with all details).

Export as CSV (loan breakdown).

Stored in Secure Vault inside app (encrypted).

Shareable secure link → expires after 24 hours.

👉 Users can easily carry their credit report to advisors or lenders.
