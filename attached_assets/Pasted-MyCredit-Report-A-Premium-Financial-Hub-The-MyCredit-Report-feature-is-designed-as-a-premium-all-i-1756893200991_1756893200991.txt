MyCredit Report: A Premium Financial Hub
The MyCredit Report feature is designed as a premium, all-in-one financial hub for loan app users. It moves beyond a simple credit score check to provide a comprehensive, interactive roadmap for financial improvement, with the goal of increasing user eligibility and loan conversion. The feature is structured around several interconnected dashboards and tools that empower users with data, insights, and actionable advice.

Key Sections & Functionality
1. Snapshot Dashboard 🎯
This is the landing page that gives users an instant, high-level overview of their financial health. It prominently displays their Current Credit Score with a trust badge, their Debt Summary (total active loans), and a Loan Eligibility Score that shows their percentage chance of approval. Quick-access buttons are available to navigate to other key sections like "View Timeline" and "Improve My Score."

2. Credit Score Timeline 📈
A dynamic, graph-based dashboard that visualizes a user's credit score history. It intelligently highlights the key reasons for past score changes, explaining why the score went up or down. A key feature is the predictive projection, which shows a user how their score could improve if they follow the recommended roadmap. This makes the often-complex credit score transparent and easy to understand.

3. Loan & Debt Dashboard 📊
This dashboard serves as a unified hub for all of a user's loans. It lists all active loans and displays important stats through graphs, such as Total Interest Paid, Remaining Principal, and a breakdown of installments (done vs. pending). A standout feature is the Personalized Loan Calculator which allows users to simulate the impact of prepayments, showing them how much they can save in interest or shorten their loan tenure.

4. Actionable Roadmap 🗺️
This section turns data into a clear plan. It provides a personalized "What to Improve" list, ranking actions by urgency and potential score impact. A step-by-step "How to Improve" timeline guides users with specific dates and projected score changes. A Visual Progress Graph illustrates how their loan eligibility will rise as they complete each action, providing powerful motivation.

5. Scenario Simulator 🧠
This is an interactive "what-if" tool that allows users to test the financial impact of their decisions before they make them. Users can input scenarios, such as repaying a loan or applying for a new credit card, and the dashboard provides instant recalculations, showing them the direct impact on their credit score and loan approval chances through live graph updates.

6. MyCoach (AI Assistant) 🤖
An AI-powered chatbot that acts as a personal credit trainer. It floats across all MyCredit pages, explaining complex financial terms in simple language, suggesting next best actions, and setting up automated reminders for things like EMI due dates.

7. Secure Export & Sharing 🔐
This feature provides users with total control over their data. They can download their report in various formats (e.g., a quick Snapshot PDF or a Full Report PDF with all details). For added security, a secure link can be generated for sharing with advisors or lenders, which automatically expires after a set period, giving users peace of mind.
