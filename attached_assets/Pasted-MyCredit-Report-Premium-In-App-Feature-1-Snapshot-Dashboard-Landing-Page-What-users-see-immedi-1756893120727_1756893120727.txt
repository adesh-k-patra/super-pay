Improve My Score

Download Report

All loans listed with duration, EMI, due dates, repayment history.

Graphs & Stats:

Total Interest Paid.

Remaining Principal.

Total Installments (done vs pending).

Missed Payments (highlighted red).

Personalized Loan Calculators:

Prepayment Impact:

“Pay ₹20,000 now → Save ₹5,200 interest + close loan 4 months earlier.”

Tenure vs EMI Calculator:

“Pay higher EMI → Close 6 months sooner.”

“Lower EMI → Pay longer but manage cash flow.”

👉 Control & clarity: No hidden surprises, full visibility of loans.

Ranked “What to Improve” list (sorted by urgency & score impact).

Example: “Clear Overdue Loan → +50 points.”

Test financial choices before acting:

Floating chatbot across all MyCredit pages.

Functions:

Explains score changes in simple terms.

Sets reminders: “Remind me 2 days before EMI due.”

Download Options:
