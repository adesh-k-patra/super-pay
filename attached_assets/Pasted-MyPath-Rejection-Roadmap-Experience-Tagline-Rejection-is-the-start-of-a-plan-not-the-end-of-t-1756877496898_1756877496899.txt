MyPath — Rejection → Roadmap Experience

Tagline: Rejection is the start of a plan, not the end of the road.

What it is (short)

A contextual roadmap that activates after a loan rejection, explaining the reasons in plain language, prioritizing fixes, and showing clickable next steps. MyPath transforms rejection into a clear action plan with simulated impact, quick product links, and progress tracking — turning failure into a guided roadmap toward approval.

Why it matters

Converts rejection into retention + cross-sell opportunity.

Reduces churn by offering clarity and hope.

Improves future approval odds with guided actions.

Upsells MyReport, Coach, and credit-builder products.

Access & Pricing

Available for all rejected loans.

Triggered via Rejection Modal.

Unlock MyPath dashboard with a ₹25 one-time purchase (per rejection event).

After purchase → dashboard is unlocked permanently for that rejection case.

Primary User Flow

Loan Application → Rejected

Rejection Modal (empathetic copy + CTA “View MyPath for ₹25”)

Payment screen → complete one-time unlock.

MyPath Dashboard → rejection reasons, score snapshot, prioritized fixes.

Recommendation Cards → each shows reason, fix, predicted impact.

Simulate Impact → sliders / inputs to test “what if” scenarios.

Quick Actions → apply for credit-builder loan, settle dues, restructure EMI.

Progress Tracker → milestones, reminders, readiness bar to reapply.

Conversion Hooks → upsell MyReport or Coach for deeper guidance.

Key Pages & Elements
1. Rejection Modal

Message: empathetic tone → “This rejection isn’t the end. Let’s make a roadmap together.”

CTA → “Unlock MyPath for ₹25”.

2. MyPath Dashboard

Top banner: user’s score + eligibility %.

Multi-cause rejection cards (bureau + lender).

Prioritized list of fixes with predicted score impact.

3. Recommendation Cards (customized per user)

Each card contains:

Reason (e.g., “Missed EMI on Loan A, June 5th”)

Action (e.g., “Clear overdue of ₹4,500”)

Impact (e.g., Estimated score +12, eligibility ↑ 8%)

Timeline (ETA for score change)

Confidence Band (low/med/high certainty)

CTA: Pay now, Settle, Apply for credit-builder, Restructure EMI.

Examples of elements to include on cards:

Due Payments: “Missed EMI on Loan A (05 June) → settle now for +10–15 points.”

Loan Cancellations: “Too many open applications. Cancel old requests for +5 points.”

Credit Utilization: “Card X utilization 78%. Reduce to 30% for +18 points.”

DTI Ratio: “High EMI/income ratio (52%). Pay off Loan Y for +12 points.”

Delinquencies: “1 overdue >90 days. Dispute or settle for +25 points.”

Account Age: “Thin file (avg. age 6m). Open secured card → +8 points.”

Inquiries: “5 hard pulls last 3 months. Avoid new apps for 6m → +7 points.”

4. Simulate Impact

Interactive slider: “If I pay ₹X on Loan Y → Projected score ↑ by Y”.

Graph: eligibility % progression across weeks.

Allow stacking scenarios (settle + reduce utilization + add secured loan).

5. Quick Actions

Direct CTAs to:

Credit-builder loan offers

EMI restructuring tools

Settlement APIs

Coach sessions

6. Progress Tracker

Visual milestones: “2 of 5 actions completed”.

Badges & streaks for motivation.

Readiness meter: “You’re 78% ready to reapply”.

7. Share & Ask

Export roadmap as secure PDF.

Share with advisor.

Book Creator 1-on-1 for personalized coaching.

Sub-Features

Rejection reason extractor: parses lender codes + adds bureau insights.

Quick wins vs long-term fixes: class fixes by ETA & impact.

Re-application guardrails: system warns if user reapplies too soon.

Auto-suggestions to MyReport: prompt upgrade if user wants weekly roadmap.

Data & Integrations

Lender rejection codes/messages (structured or parsed text).

Bureau + open banking data (from MyReport).

Product APIs: credit-builder, settlement, restructuring.

Privacy & Compliance

Store rejection reasons only if user consents.

Allow deletion from settings.

No hard blocking of re-application (advisory only).

KPIs

Re-application conversion rate after MyPath.

Uptake of cross-sell products from MyPath.

Churn reduction (immediate drop-off post-rejection).

NPS improvement among rejected users.

Acceptance Criteria

Rejection modal always appears post-loan rejection.

Modal contains single-tap unlock (₹25 purchase).

Dashboard must show at least 3 prioritized fixes with predicted impacts.

Each fix has score impact estimate + CTA.

Simulator works with at least 2 variables.

Progress tracker reflects completed vs pending actions.

⚡ In short: MyPath converts every rejection into a paid, personalized roadmap. For just ₹25, the user gets a clear list of reasons, fixes, simulated impacts, progress tracking, and direct product links — while also driving retention and upsells.
