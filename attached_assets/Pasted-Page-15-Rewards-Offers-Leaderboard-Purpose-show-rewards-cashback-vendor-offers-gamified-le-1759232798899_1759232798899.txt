Buy & Sell UX rules (must-have)
* Always show From account and To account before final confirm.
* Show UPI payee VPA prominently in checkout.
* For Market orders show executed price after fill; for Limit show expected window & TTL.
* Offer slippage tolerance control & auto-cancel on excess slippage.
* Support partial fills and show fill breakdown with timestamps.
* Immediate toast + push notification on execution.
* Maintain audit logs (order id → fills → payment txn id).
* For vendor marketplace: show vendor inventory & expiration of locked quotes.
