Pick & Drop / Courier Delivery feature (think Porter / Lalamove / Dunzo courier) you can hand straight to PMs, designers, and devs. I kept it practical and developer-ready, with page-level requirements, flows, data examples, edge cases, and KPIs. 1 — One-line product summary Pick & Drop — on-demand and scheduled courier deliveries for parcels, furniture, groceries, and heavy items with real-time tracking, vehicle selection, instant pricing, proof-of-delivery, insurance options and business tools for enterprises. 2 — Core value propositions Instant same-hour local pickups and drops (on-demand). Scheduled pickups (hour/day/week). Multiple vehicle types (bike, 2-ton/mini truck, tempo, van) with accurate capacity & pricing. Transparent pricing, live ETA, and driver assignment optimised for proximity & capacity. Proof of delivery (photo, OTP/RTO signature), insurance & COD support. 3 — Primary user journeys Quick Booking (On-demand): User selects pickup & drop addresses → choose vehicle → see instant fare & ETA → book → driver assigned → real-time tracking → POD & rating. Scheduled Booking: User selects date/time, optionally multiple stops → confirm fare → schedule → driver assignment close to slot. Bulk / B2B: Upload CSV of jobs or API integration for recurring logistics. Driver flow: Accept/Reject jobs → navigation → pickup confirmation → upload pickup proof → deliver → POD → earnings reconciliation. Admin/OPS: View live jobs, reassign drivers, refunds, SLA alerts, driver performance. 4 — Supported deliveries & constraints Item types: documents, small parcels, groceries, furniture, appliances, bike/car deliveries (requires larger vehicles/partner). Max weight per vehicle type (configurable): Bike 0–5 kg, Auto 0–150 kg, Mini Truck 0–800 kg, 2T 800–2000 kg. Max dimensions per vehicle. Dangerous goods: blocked or special permit only (no flammables, hazardous chemicals). Insurance: optional value-based insurance selection during checkout. 5 — Pricing & fare model Fare components: Base fare (vehicle type) + distance fare (per km) + time/idle charges + size/weight surcharge + priority/express fee + toll & taxes + COD fee (if applicable). Surge: dynamic multiplier based on demand & driver availability. Min fare: per city/vehicle. Estimation engine: route distance (routing API), estimated time, traffic factor. Promos & Wallets: support coupons, corporate credits, monthly billing for enterprise. Invoice: GST, breakdown for B2B. 6 — Booking page (UI + functional requirements) Input UI: Pickup address (auto-detect & saved addresses) + contact name & phone. Drop address(es) (support multi-stop up to N stops). Item summary: item type (parcel/furniture), weight (manual or estimated), dimensions (L/W/H optional), photos upload at booking (optional). Vehicle selector: icons + capacity + ETA + fare preview. Default smart suggestion based on item volume/weight. Service type: On-demand (ASAP) / Scheduled (date & time window) / Priority (faster). Additional options: Assisted loading/unloading, two-person handling, lift access required, wait time allowance, insurance coverage, COD collection (amount + receiver details). Notes & delivery instructions. Fare preview: show itemized cost, ETA window, driver arrival ETA, cancellation fee notice. Confirm booking CTA (fixed bottom button). Validation: address geocode verification, phone format, required fields. 7 — Booking confirmation & payment After confirm: show Job ID, estimated driver ETAs, rider details (masked phone), order summary, ability to cancel within free window. Payment options: prepaid (UPI/cards/wallets), COD (postpaid), corporate account (invoice). Immediate payment flow (if prepaid): payment sheet sliding up, PIN / OTP flows, success & failure UX. Booking email/SMS/Push receipt with tracking link. 8 — Live tracking & status page (UI + features) Real-time status states: Requested → Driver Assigned → Enroute to Pickup → Arrived at Pickup → Picked Up → Enroute to Drop → Arrived at Drop → Delivered → Closed. Live map with driver icon, route polyline, pickup & drop pins, live ETA, and distance remaining. Driver card: name, vehicle type, plate number, driver photo, masked phone (tap-to-call via masked relay). Timeline view below map with time stamps and status. Actions: Chat with driver (in-app), call driver (masked), change instructions (before pickup only), cancel (if allowed). Share tracking link (public short link). 9 — Proof of pickup & delivery (POD) Pickup confirmation: driver marks “Arrived” and uploads photo of goods at pickup location or courier scans pre-booked QR / barcode. Optional sender OTP verification. Delivery confirmation: choose one or more POD methods: Receiver OTP (driver requests OTP from recipient) Photo capture of delivered goods at location (with geo-tag) Electronic signature (draw or typed name) Name entered by driver + optional ID photo for high value Auto-attachment of POD to job record and to customer invoice/email
