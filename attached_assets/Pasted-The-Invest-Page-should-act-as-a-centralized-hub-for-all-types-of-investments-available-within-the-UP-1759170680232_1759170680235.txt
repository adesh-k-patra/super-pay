The Invest Page should act as a centralized hub for all types of investments available within the UPI app. At the top, users can select their funding source (“From Account”) and destination (“To Investment”), making transactions seamless. The categories should include Gold, Silver, Bronze, Diamond, Platinum, Stocks, Mutual Funds, and other possible investment products. Each category should open to a detailed page displaying current live prices, historical price charts, seller details, risk level, potential returns, and descriptive insights. Similar to stock market apps, the page must include Buy and Sell options, along with order history and a Rewards page to track benefits.

Each investment detail page should be well structured, showing key KPIs such as price history (daily, weekly, yearly), volatility index, risk category, expected returns, and seller/provider information. For precious metals (Gold, Silver, etc.), the detail page should allow buying/selling in units or amounts with real-time price updates. For Mutual Funds, a separate detail page is needed with NAV, fund type, past performance charts, holdings, fund manager details, and category risk. For Stocks, users should see market price, day’s high/low, charts, company description, financial ratios, and order options (Market, Limit, Stop Loss).

The AI Invest feature should provide a smart option for users who want guided investing. Here, users simply enter the investment amount, preferred risk level (Low, Moderate, High), and investment duration. The system then automatically suggests an optimized portfolio across multiple categories such as metals, stocks, and mutual funds. It should also display a projected return range, associated risks, and diversification strategy, with an option to accept and proceed with a single click. This AI-powered recommendation engine ensures accessibility even for new investors.

The Buying & Orders section should provide a complete transaction experience. When buying, users should see the option to enter buy price, mode (Market or Limit), quantity/amount, UPI payment option, and confirmation with time & date stamp. The Orders tab should track all purchases, pending orders, and executed transactions with details like invested amount, purchase price, and current market value. The Sell flow should allow users to view holdings, choose quantity to sell, confirm sale price, and instantly calculate profit/loss (including today’s profit and total profit). A comprehensive Stats Dashboard should display portfolio summary, total holdings, today’s gain/loss, overall returns, and historical performance charts to give users a clear picture of their investments.
