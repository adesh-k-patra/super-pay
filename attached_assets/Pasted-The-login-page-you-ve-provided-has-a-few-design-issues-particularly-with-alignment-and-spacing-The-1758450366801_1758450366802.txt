The login page you've provided has a few design issues, particularly with alignment and spacing. The main problems are that elements aren't properly centered, and there's a lack of consistent padding and margins, making the layout feel unbalanced and unpolished.

Issues and Fixes
Issue: Off-Center Elements misalignment of the text and input field, which don't appear to be truly centered on the page.

Fix: Use flexbox or grid layouts to ensure all primary content—like the "Welcome Back" heading, mobile number input, and "Send OTP" button—is perfectly centered, both horizontally and vertically.

Issue: Inconsistent Spacing There's not enough vertical space between the different elements, like the heading and the mobile number field, making them feel cramped.

Fix: Add more vertical spacing between each UI element (heading, input field, button) to give the design room to breathe. Use consistent margins and padding, ensuring a uniform look and feel across the entire page.

Issue: Unbalanced Layout The footer section, with its red background and customer trust information, feels disconnected from the login form above. The logo at the top also seems to be a separate element rather than a cohesive part of the header.

Fix: Create a more integrated design by using a consistent color palette and typography. The footer could have a smaller, less obtrusive design, or the login section could be enclosed within a card-like container to better define its boundaries. The overall design could also be better balanced by adjusting the size and position of the logo and header text.

Issue: No Clear Hierarchy The visual hierarchy isn't strong. The call to action ("Send OTP") doesn't stand out as much as it could, and the trust elements in the footer are quite prominent, potentially distracting from the main task of logging in.

Fix: Highlight the primary call to action by making the "Send OTP" button more visually distinct, perhaps with a brighter color or a stronger shadow. The trust elements should be present but less dominant, perhaps using a more subtle background color or smaller icons.
