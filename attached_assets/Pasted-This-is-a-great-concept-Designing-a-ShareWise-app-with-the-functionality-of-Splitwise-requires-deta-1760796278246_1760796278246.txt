Search bar, Add Contact button (e.g., a simple '+' icon).Profile ManagementView and edit user details (name, photo, currency, password).Settings icon (gear). Clean, single-screen profile view with clear edit options.Black & White Mode ToggleA dedicated setting to switch the entire application to the requested modern black and white theme (high contrast, crisp lines).Prominent Toggle Switch in Settings (if an alternate theme is ever introduced).Push NotificationsAlerts for new expenses, payments received, or settlement requests.Automatic system integration.2. Group Management (The Groups Tab)This section focuses on managing shared expense environments. This is the main screen upon opening the app.FeatureDescriptionUI Element/ActionAll Groups OverviewA list of all groups the user is currently a member of.List view with group name, a summary of user's net balance (e.g., "You owe $45" or "You are owed $120"), and a small recent activity snippet.Create GroupStart a new expense group with a name and initial members.Prominent 'Create Group' button (or a large '+' icon) on the main Groups screen.Delete Group(Admin Feature) Remove a group permanently. Only possible after all balances are settled to zero.Group settings menu 'Delete Group' button (Requires a confirmation step).Add/Remove MemberModify the participant list for an existing group.Group View Page: Dedicated 'Members' section with 'Add Member' and 'Remove' icons next to each member's name.Group SettingsEdit group name, photo, and default currency.Settings icon (gear) within the Group View.3. Expense Tracking & Splitting (The Group View Page)This is the most critical and frequently used part of the application, seen after tapping on a group from the list.FeatureDescriptionUI Element/ActionTransaction TimelineA chronological list of all expenses added to the group.Main content area of the Group View. Camera icon for receipt upload.Edit/Delete TransactionModify or remove a transaction after it has been created.Tap on a transaction card to open details, then an Edit/Delete button in the corner.4. Analytics & Overview (The Group View Page)Provides users with a clear understanding of their current financial standing within the group.FeatureDescriptionUI Element/ActionOverall Balance SummaryThe most important data point.
