The app then asks for details like their PAN card number, date of birth, and monthly income to instantly check their eligibility for a loan. This step is a quick check to ensure the user qualifies for a loan product before they invest more time in the application.

KYC & Document Submission: If the user is eligible, they proceed to complete their Know Your Customer (KYC) process. This is often done digitally through an Aadhaar-based e-KYC, where the user can submit a digital copy of their Aadhaar and PAN card. They may also need to upload a recent selfie and provide bank statements or salary slips to verify their income.

Loan Offer & Customization: Based on the submitted information, the system generates a personalized loan offer. The user can then select their desired loan amount and repayment tenure. This step often includes a built-in EMI calculator so the user can see how their choices affect their monthly payments, allowing them to customize the loan to their financial comfort.

Verification & Final Submission: The application then goes through a final verification process. The user may be required to sign the loan agreement electronically using a one-time password (OTP) or an Aadhaar-based e-sign. This is the last step before the application is officially submitted.

Application Submitted Confirmation Page (The Fix): This is the crucial final page that your current process is missing. After the final submission, the app should display a "Congratulations" page. This page should be well-designed, with a clear headline like "Loan Application Submitted!" and a message that sets the user's expectations for what happens next. The message should clearly state that the application is under review and mention the expected timeframe for a decision, for example, "Your loan application has been successfully submitted and is being reviewed. We will notify you of the final decision within 3-7 working days." It's also helpful to provide a reference number and a clear way to track the application's status.
